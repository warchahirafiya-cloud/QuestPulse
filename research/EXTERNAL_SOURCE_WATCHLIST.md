## Mining apps and sites

**No mining app/site is approved for listing in this review.** “Mining” may describe a points game, ad-reward app, simulated balance, or cloud-mining contract rather than independently verifiable blockchain mining. Do not publish a mining link until the operator, actual mechanism, payout route, fees, withdrawal proof, and security risks have been checked. Never treat a displayed in-app balance as a withdrawable asset.

## Mandatory checks before moving any candidate into `data/opportunities.json`

1. Confirm the exact official domain from first-party documentation or a verified project channel; reject clones and URL shorteners.
2. Record a dated source URL and a second independent verification source.
3. Confirm whether the campaign is open, ended, waitlisted, snapshot-only, or claim-only.
4. Record eligibility restrictions, tasks, deposit/activation requirements, withdrawal minimum, asset, network, fees, and reset interval only when explicitly documented.
5. Verify payment evidence with transaction references where possible. Label community screenshots as reports, not proof.
6. Review wallet signatures, token approvals, custody, smart-contract risks, liquidity, FDV, vesting, and unlocks where relevant.
7. Keep the record `unverified` until all publication gates are satisfied. Never imply guaranteed rewards.

## Publication and legal boundary

The project's current `docs/EDITORIAL_POLICY.md` and `docs/PROJECT_DOSSIER.md` require written legal review before publishing live participation links, referral links, bot invitations, deposit instructions, or monetized promotions. This watchlist therefore stays outside the public opportunity feed and does not authorize promotion or participation. Do not merge candidate links into public cards until the legal and editorial gates are cleared.
