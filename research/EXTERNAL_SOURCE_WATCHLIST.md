## Mandatory checks before moving any candidate into `data/opportunities.json`

1. Confirm the exact official domain from first-party documentation or a verified project channel; reject clones and URL shorteners.
2. Record a dated source URL and a second independent verification source.
3. Confirm whether the campaign is open, ended, waitlisted, snapshot-only, or claim-only.
4. Record eligibility restrictions, tasks, deposit/activation requirements, withdrawal minimum, asset, network, fees, and reset interval only when explicitly documented.
5. Verify payment evidence with transaction references where possible. Label community screenshots as reports, not proof.
6. Review wallet signatures, token approvals, custody, smart-contract risks, liquidity, FDV, vesting, and unlocks where relevant.
7. Keep the record `unverified` until all publication gates are satisfied. Never imply guaranteed rewards.

### Important distinctions discovered

- **Points are not tokens:** Cashwalk, Mani, Playio, PLAYUS and similar services describe loyalty points, gift cards, mobile data or products; do not label these as cryptocurrency.
- **Prize draws are not guaranteed income:** aruku&, POINTs and ANA Pocket describe drawings/gacha-style rewards.
- **A token mention is not a payout guarantee:** a game may have a token, NFT or staking feature while free users cannot withdraw anything.
- **Past events stay expired:** the MiL.k × The Sandbox campaign above is marked over and must never appear as active.
- **Regional lock matters:** many Japanese and Korean reward apps depend on local stores, phone numbers, app-store regions, KYC or local redemption catalogues.
- **No app is marked verified by this scan:** every entry remains a research lead until first-party terms, current availability, actual withdrawal requirements, and independent evidence are checked.
