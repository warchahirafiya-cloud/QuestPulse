# QuestPulse Editorial and Publication Policy

**Purpose:** Publish accurate, dated, source-backed informational records without implying endorsement, guaranteed rewards, or universal eligibility.

## Current publication boundary
The project dossier records a legal operating constraint that requires qualified written legal advice before launching features that may promote, facilitate, or monetize virtual-asset activities. Until that review is complete, the public site must remain a neutral informational and educational prototype. Do not add live participation links, referral links, Telegram bot invitations, deposit instructions, paid activation paths, or monetized promotions.

This policy does not determine the legal status of any feature. Obtain advice from qualified counsel on the exact operating model and proposed content before expanding beyond neutral educational material.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Official primary documentation or an official project announcement, checked against the authentic domain/account.
2. Public on-chain records or independently verifiable transaction references, where relevant and lawfully appropriate.
3. Reputable independent reporting or technical analysis.
4. Community reports and screenshots as contextual leads only, never as sole proof of safety or ongoing payments.

## Required distinctions
- **Fact:** directly supported by a cited source.
- **Reported:** attributed to a named source or user report.
- **Estimate:** calculated or inferred; explain assumptions.
- **Unknown:** not established; do not fill by guessing.

## Minimum review record
For any record that is eventually approved for publication, preserve:
- Stable record ID and category.
- Official source and independent verification sources.
- Review timestamp and reviewer notes.
- Eligibility and geographic restrictions, if lawfully publishable and verified.
- Tasks, fees, deposits, withdrawal minimum, payout asset, network and fees, only where confirmed.
- Reset/check-in interval, only where confirmed.
- Evidence limitations and unresolved concerns.
- Risk level with a short rationale.

## Status rules
- unverified: not enough evidence.
- under_review: active editorial checks; not an endorsement.
- verified: required source, date and material terms checks completed. This is not a safety or payout guarantee.
- paused / expired: do not present as active.
- Availability remains unknown unless there is a current, dated basis for a different status.

## Risk and security review
Check impersonation, suspicious domains, seed phrase/private key requests, wallet signatures, token approvals, custody, activation fees, withdrawal-unlock fees, unclear fee schedules, liquidity, FDV, vesting and token unlocks where relevant and legally appropriate.

Never request or publish private keys, recovery phrases, passwords, authentication codes, personal identity documents, or private account information.

## Corrections and freshness
- Re-check time-sensitive records before presenting them as current.
- Preserve a dated audit trail of material changes.
- Correct errors transparently and downgrade status when evidence becomes stale or contradictory.
- A payment screenshot is not proof of future payments or general eligibility.

## Editorial independence
No record may be marked verified because of a referral relationship, payment, sponsorship, or request from a project. Any future commercial relationship requires separate legal review and clear disclosure if legally permitted.
